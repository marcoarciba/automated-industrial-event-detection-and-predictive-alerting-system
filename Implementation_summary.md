# Implementation Summary

Due to intellectual property restrictions, client confidentiality agreements, and proprietary industrial analytics methodologies, production code, deployment artifacts, and operational datasets cannot be publicly shared.

This repository focuses on documenting:

- Business problem
- Analytical architecture
- Methodology evolution
- Forecasting framework
- Root-cause investigation workflow
- Predictive alerting methodology

The repository is intended to showcase the analytical design and engineering approach behind the solution while protecting confidential business information.
